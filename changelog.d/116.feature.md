Programs that enable the business or strategy companion repository now get a
generated per-repository guide for its owning agent, in place of one written
by hand per program.

The guide states who owns the repository, which direction work flows between
the two companions, and who may read which paths. Where a repository declares
no sensitivity classes at all, the guide says that nobody has classified it
yet rather than leaving the section out, since an absent section reads as
nothing here being sensitive. A path declared sensitive but given no audience
is shown as unclassified rather than defaulting to anything reassuring.

Private orchestration rules stay out of this file, as they do everywhere else:
it is committed, and the guide points at the generated local file that holds
them.

A skill-pack path that points inside an OpenSpec change directory is flagged
in the generated guide, because such a path stops working when that change is
archived.
