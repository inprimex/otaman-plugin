Generated security workflows now carry the per-layer time budget from
configuration.

The budget was being resolved and then dropped, because the templates had no
field for it. They now do, named in seconds to match the resolved value, so
it passes through without conversion — a budget of 300 seconds landing in a
field read as minutes would have become five hours.

Where no budget is configured, the generated workflow says so in the way the
template expects, leaving its own default to apply.
