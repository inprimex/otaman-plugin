Review gates can now be assembled from the project's configured selection
policies, with every layer of the cofounder review stack accounted for.

Each layer reports either that it ran and who performed it, or that it did
not run and why. Where a sensitivity classification excludes every eligible
reviewer, the layer names the reviewers excluded and the clearance they
lacked, rather than appearing simply to have found nothing. A stack with any
layer unrun is reported as incomplete, so it cannot be presented as a full
review.

The rules deciding who reviews what live in one shared implementation
rather than being restated here.
