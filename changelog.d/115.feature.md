Stage-2 critic selection now resolves through the declared gate policies in
`verification-gates.yaml` instead of a rule private to the dispatcher, and the
same file is now where the security-gate configuration is read from.

A proposal's author is never selected to review it. That exclusion now applies
to every policy rather than being re-derived per caller, which closes a case
where an author who owned one of the affected repositories was selected to
review their own proposal.

Where the selection cannot be made, the dispatcher states which of the
situations it is in — no gate configuration, no policy for this gate, an
installation that cannot apply the author exclusion, or a policy that selected
nobody. On an installation that cannot apply the exclusion no critique is
dispatched at all, rather than one that could reach the author. A proposal is
never held up by any of this: it reaches the human queue on the deterministic
result alone.

Configuration declared in both the old and new location is reported rather
than silently resolved, so edits to the retired location cannot quietly stop
taking effect.
